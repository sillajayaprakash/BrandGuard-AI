Brand Guideline Reference

Enterprise brand voice, tone principles, and regulatory boundaries.

🎯 Core Brand Tone Objective

Authoritative, consultative, trustworthy, and solution-focused.

✅ Approved Best Practices (DO)

  • Substantiate claims with specific SLA percentages or verified metrics.
  • Use customer-centric language emphasizing long-term partnerships.
  • State technical capabilities transparently and accurately.
  • Maintain an inviting, respectful, and consultative tone.

❌ Strictly Prohibited (DON'T)

  • Never make absolute guarantees (e.g. "100% risk-free", "world's best").
  • Avoid high-pressure urgency ("ACT NOW OR REGRET", "LIMITED TIME").
  • Do not use depreciative terms like "cheap" or dismissive colloquialisms.
  • Never promise specific financial or medical outcomes without disclaimers.
